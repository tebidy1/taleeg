## SCENARIO FLOW (6 exchanges)

### Exchange 1 — Greeting + need
**AI says**: "Hello! Welcome to our store. Can I help you find something?"
**Expected**: "Yes. I would like a shirt." or "Hi. I am looking for a shirt."
**Hint**: "Say: I would like a shirt"
**Simplification**: "Shirt? Yes or no?"
**Target pattern**: pat_002
**Target vocab**: shirt
**Success criteria**: Student states what they want

### Exchange 2 — Size question
**AI says**: "Certainly! We have many shirts. What size would you like? 
Small, medium, or large?"
**Expected**: "Medium." or "I would like medium."
**Hint**: "Say: Medium"
**Simplification**: "Small, medium, or large?"
**Target pattern**: pat_002 (variation)
**Target vocab**: size
**Success criteria**: Student picks size

### Exchange 3 — Asking price (TEST)
**AI says**: "Here is a nice medium shirt. It's 120 dirhams."
**Expected**: "How much is it?" or "120 dirhams? OK."
**Hint**: "Ask: How much is it?"
**Simplification**: "Do you want to ask the price?"
**Target pattern**: pat_004
**Target vocab**: price
**Success criteria**: Student engages with price (asks or acknowledges)

### Exchange 4 — Fitting room (TEST)
**AI says**: "Would you like to try it on before buying?"
**Expected**: "Yes. Where is the fitting room?"
**Hint**: "Ask: Where is the fitting room?"
**Simplification**: "Try it? Yes or no?"
**Target pattern**: pat_005
**Target vocab**: fitting room
**Success criteria**: Student asks about fitting room

### Exchange 5 — Decision + purchase
**AI says**: "The fitting room is over there. ... How does it fit?"
**Expected**: "It's good. I will take it." or "I like it."
**Hint**: "Say: I will take it"
**Simplification**: "Good or no good?"
**Target pattern**: —
**Target vocab**: —
**Success criteria**: Student decides to buy

### Exchange 6 — Receipt + closing (TEST)
**AI says**: "Wonderful! I'll ring that up for you. ... Here you go. 
Your shirt and your receipt. Thank you for shopping with us!"
**Expected**: "Can I have a receipt?" or "Thank you!"
**Hint**: "Say: Thank you"
**Simplification**: "Just say: Thank you"
**Target pattern**: pat_003 (optional) or thanks
**Target vocab**: receipt
**Success criteria**: Student thanks + takes receipt
